Mergeheim: Tower Defense — Privacy Policy

← Mateo Silva Games

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy explains what information Mergeheim: Tower Defense ("the game") uses. The game is developed by Mateo Silva (Mateo Silva Games) ("we"). Contact: mateosilvagames@gmail.com.

Your game progress

The game has no user accounts and we do not ask for your email or any contact details. Your progress (units, levels, coins, gems, pass, achievements and settings) is stored on your device. Deleting the game deletes this data.

Online play (co-op and PvP)

Solo matches are played on your device. When you play online co-op or PvP, the game connects to our game server (hosted on Cloudflare) to find another player and keep both matches in sync. For this it sends: the player name, avatar and title you chose; your trophies; your unit, talisman and Path levels and champion skins (so both devices run the same match); the game version; and your in-match actions. The other player sees your name, avatar, title and trophies. Player names are checked against a filter of offensive words. Match data is kept only while the match is running. Like any internet service, the server processes your IP address to connect; we do not use it to identify you.

Chat, reports and moderation

During online matches you can send quick messages or short texts to the other player. Messages are filtered for offensive words, links, emails and phone numbers. You can mute a player, report a player, and turn chat off in Settings. Messages are not stored after the match, except when someone sends a report: the report keeps the players' names, the reason and that match's conversation so we can review it. We review reports and act on them within 24 hours, and keep them for up to 12 months. To report a problem or ask us to delete a report about you, write to mateosilvagames@gmail.com.

Mailbox, season and game updates

The game downloads news, mail with gifts and the Season Pass dates from our server; these requests do not include personal information. The game may also download updates of its own content through Expo (EAS Update), which receives technical information such as the app version and platform to deliver them (expo.dev/privacy).

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game can show optional rewarded video ads, for example to get a free chest. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect device information such as the advertising identifier, IP address, device model, operating system and ad interactions. Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice. On iOS, the game asks for permission before using the advertising identifier; if you decline, ads are not personalized. Google's use of this data is described in its privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In-app purchases

Purchases (gem and coin packs, the Season Pass and the VIP Pack) are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your payment details. To validate purchases and deliver the items, the game uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user identifier, the purchased product and the transaction receipt. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

Random rewards

Chests contain random rewards. The odds of each type of reward are shown in the game before you buy a chest (the "Odds" button).

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Online chat can be turned off in Settings.

Your choices

You can play solo without going online, turn chat off, mute or report players, reset your advertising identifier or limit ad tracking in your device settings, decline the tracking permission on iOS, and change your ad consent from the game (Settings). You can delete all game data on your device by uninstalling the game, and ask us by email to delete any report that mentions you.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
